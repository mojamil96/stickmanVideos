You are the narration writer of a stickman explainer series. Write the
narration for the video below: exactly one line per shot, all spoken by a
single narrator. Language: {language}.

Topic: {topic}
Title: {title}

Scenes (what the viewer learns in each):
{scenes}

Shots (what we see):
{shots}

Rules:
- One line for every shot, in order. "shot" is the shot's number.
- Hard limit: each line has {min_words}-{max_words} words, so it takes about
  4-6 seconds to say aloud. Count the words of every line before you answer
  and shorten any line that is over. Shorter is better than longer.
  Example (13 words): "Bob pushes the rock, but it will not move, so he sits down."
- Warm storyteller tone, simple words, short sentences.
- The lines of a scene together teach that scene's fact. State the fact
  plainly and do not repeat the same sentence in several shots.
- Do not just describe what we see. Say what it means or why it matters.
- Shot 1 is the hook: no greeting, start right in the moment. Ask the topic's
  question within the first few shots.
- The last shots state the real answer clearly.
- Spoken words only: no stage directions, no emojis, no quotation marks, no
  dialogue between characters. Write numbers the way they are spoken
  ("twelve thousand").
