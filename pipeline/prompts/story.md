You are the director of a stickman explainer series. Make the story for one
complete video about the topic below, fully on your own.

Main character: {name}. {description}
Topic: {topic}

Tell it as a story, not a list of facts:
- Scene 1 is the hook: the most surprising moment of the story, shown before
  the question is asked.
  It must create a question the viewer wants answered.
- {name} wants something concrete (for example a place to live, or finding
  food). Every scene moves that goal forward or blocks it, something goes
  wrong along the way, and the surprising answer at the end resolves it.
- Every scene should make the viewer want the next one.
- Exactly {scene_count} scenes. The finished video runs
  {min_seconds}-{max_seconds} seconds.
- Each scene has two parts: "visual" (one or two sentences about what we see)
  and "fact" (the one thing the viewer learns). Keep them separate: no facts,
  dates or explanations in "visual", no actions in "fact".
- Every fact is a true statement (never a question or something a character
  wants) and is new: do not repeat a fact from an earlier scene.
- Each scene happens in one place and has one main event, plus at most one
  small follow-up.
- The title is a curious question or promise that names the topic.

Accuracy:
- The answer must be historically or scientifically accurate. Do not invent
  facts. If experts disagree, tell the version most experts accept.
- The last scene must state the real answer to the topic's question clearly.

Visual rules:
- {name}'s look, the drawing style and the background style never change.
  You only choose his outfit.
- All characters are stickman humans. No animals as characters; show animals
  only as a distant mention, never as actors.
- Props and scenery stay very simple (rocks, sticks, a fire, a tree).
- Describe the outfit in natural words ("his helmet"), never as field names.
- Everything must be showable with simple stickman movement: walking, waving,
  jumping, pointing, sitting, falling.
- No on-screen text: no captions, titles, signs or notes. Text is added later.

  hats: {hats}
  accessories: {accessories}
